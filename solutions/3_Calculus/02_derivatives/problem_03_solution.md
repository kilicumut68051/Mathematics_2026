## Question

For

$$
f(x)=(x^2+1)e^x
$$

compute the derivative. Then compare the correct result with the incorrect idea

$$
f'(x)\stackrel{?}{=}(2x)e^x
$$

and explain which term is missing.

## Solution

Using the product rule $(uv)' = u'v + uv'$ with $u = x^2 + 1$ and $v = e^x$:

$$
f'(x) = (2x)e^x + (x^2 + 1)e^x = (x^2 + 2x + 1)e^x
$$

The incorrect expression $(2x)e^x$ omits the second product term $(x^2 + 1)e^x$, which accounts for the derivative of $e^x$ multiplied by $x^2 + 1$.
