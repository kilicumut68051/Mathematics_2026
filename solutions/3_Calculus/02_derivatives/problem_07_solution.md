## Question

For

$$
f(x)=\sin(x^2+1)
$$

compute the derivative. Explicitly identify the outer function, the inner function, and the derivative of the inner function. Finally, determine the sign of $f'(0)$.

## Solution

Outer function: $\sin(u)$ (derivative $\cos(u)$).
Inner function: $u = x^2 + 1$.
Derivative of inner function: $u'(x) = 2x$.

By the chain rule:

$$
f'(x) = 2x\cos(x^2 + 1)
$$

At $x = 0$:

$$
f'(0) = 2(0)\cos(1) = 0
$$

Since $f'(0) = 0$, it is neither positive nor negative (zero).
