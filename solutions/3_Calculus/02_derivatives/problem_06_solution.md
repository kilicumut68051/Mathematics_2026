## Question

For

$$
f(x)=x\sin x+\cos x
$$

compute the derivative and simplify the result. Then compute $f'(0)$ and interpret the direction of the tangent line at that point.

## Solution

Applying the product rule to $x\sin x$ and differentiating $\cos x$:

$$
f'(x) = (1\cdot\sin x + x\cos x) - \sin x = x\cos x
$$

At $x = 0$:

$$
f'(0) = 0 \cdot \cos(0) = 0
$$

Since $f'(0) = 0$, the tangent line to the graph at $x = 0$ is horizontal (slope $0$).
