## Question

For

$$f(x)=e^{2x-3}+5^x$$

compute the derivative. Explain why the factor $2$ appears in the first term and why $\ln5$ appears in the second.

## Solution

$$f'(x) = 2e^{2x-3} + 5^x\ln 5$$

The factor $2$ comes from the chain rule applied to the inner exponent $2x - 3$ whose derivative is $2$. The factor $\ln 5$ appears because $(a^x)' = a^x\ln a$ for base $a = 5 \neq e$.
