## Question

For

$$
f(x)=(x^2+1)^3e^x
$$

compute the derivative. Before calculating, write down a plan: which rule applies to the whole expression and which rule applies to one of its factors. Finally, organize the result by factoring out common factors.

## Solution

Plan: Apply the product rule to $(x^2+1)^3 \cdot e^x$, and apply the chain rule when differentiating $(x^2+1)^3$.

$$
f'(x) = 3(x^2+1)^2(2x)e^x + (x^2+1)^3e^x = (x^2+1)^2e^x\bigl[6x + (x^2+1)\bigr] = (x^2+1)^2(x^2 + 6x + 1)e^x
$$
