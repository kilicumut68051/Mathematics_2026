## Question

Analyze the monotonicity and extrema of

$$f(x)=x^3-3x^2+2.$$

Sketch the graph based on the results and mark the critical points.

## Solution

$$f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6x = 3x(x - 2)$$

Critical points: $f'(x) = 0 \implies x = 0$ and $x = 2$.

- On $(-\infty, 0)$: $f'(x) > 0 \implies f$ is increasing.
- On $(0, 2)$: $f'(x) < 0 \implies f$ is decreasing.
- On $(2, \infty)$: $f'(x) > 0 \implies f$ is increasing.

Extrema:
- Local maximum at $x = 0$: $f(0) = 2$, point $(0, 2)$.
- Local minimum at $x = 2$: $f(2) = 8 - 12 + 2 = -2$, point $(2, -2)$.
