## Question

Compute

$$
\lim_{x\to4}\frac{\sqrt{x}-2}{x-4}
$$

by rationalizing the numerator. Compare the idea with Exercise 3 involving a sequence.

## Solution

$$
\lim_{x\to4}\frac{\sqrt{x}-2}{x-4} = \lim_{x\to4}\frac{(\sqrt{x}-2)(\sqrt{x}+2)}{(x-4)(\sqrt{x}+2)} = \lim_{x\to4}\frac{x-4}{(x-4)(\sqrt{x}+2)} = \lim_{x\to4}\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}+2} = \frac{1}{2+2} = \frac{1}{4}
$$

Both use a conjugate expression to eliminate an indeterminate form ($\frac{0}{0}$ here versus $\infty - \infty$ in Exercise 3) and cancel a vanishing term.
