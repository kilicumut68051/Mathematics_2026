## Question

Analyze

$$
\left(\frac23\right)^n,\qquad \left(\frac32\right)^n,\qquad (-1)^n,\qquad \frac{(-1)^n}{n}.
$$

For each sequence, describe the type of behavior and justify your answer.

## Solution

1. $\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^n$: Converges to $0$ because it is a geometric sequence with common ratio $|r| = \frac{2}{3} < 1$.
2. $\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^n$: Diverges to $+\infty$ because it is a geometric sequence with common ratio $r = \frac{3}{2} > 1$.
3. $(-1)^n$: Diverges by oscillation because it alternates between $-1$ and $1$ without approaching a single value.
4. $\frac{(-1)^n}{n}$: Converges to $0$ because $-\frac{1}{n} \le \frac{(-1)^n}{n} \le \frac{1}{n}$ and $\pm\frac{1}{n} \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$.
