## Question

Find $a$ so that

$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
ax+1,&x<2, \\
x^2-1,&x\ge2
\end{cases}
$$

is continuous at $x=2$. Explicitly compare the left-hand limit, right-hand limit, and function value.

## Solution

$$
\lim_{x\to2^-}f(x) = 2a + 1, \qquad \lim_{x\to2^+}f(x) = 2^2 - 1 = 3, \qquad f(2) = 2^2 - 1 = 3
$$

For continuity at $x = 2$, all three must be equal: $2a + 1 = 3 \implies a = 1$.
