## Question

Choose one sequence and one function from this set. For the sequence, compute several values for increasingly large $n$. For the function, prepare a table of values with arguments approaching the limit point from the left and right. Sketch the behavior of both objects.

Compare the observation with the analytical result and explain why a finite number of calculations or a sketch does not constitute a proof that the limit exists.

## Solution

Sequence: $a_n = \frac{4n^2-3n+1}{2n^2+5n-7}$ (analytical limit: $2$):
- $n = 10$: $\frac{371}{243} \approx 1.527$
- $n = 100$: $\frac{39701}{20493} \approx 1.937$
- $n = 1000$: $\frac{3997001}{2004993} \approx 1.994$

Function: $f(x) = \frac{x^2-4x+3}{x-3}$ as $x \to 3$ (analytical limit: $2$):
- From left: $x = 2.9 \to 1.9$, $x = 2.99 \to 1.99$, $x = 2.999 \to 1.999$
- From right: $x = 3.1 \to 2.1$, $x = 3.01 \to 2.01$, $x = 3.001 \to 2.001$

Numerical tables and sketches only sample finitely many points with finite precision and cannot prove behavior across all arbitrarily small neighborhoods or all $n$.
