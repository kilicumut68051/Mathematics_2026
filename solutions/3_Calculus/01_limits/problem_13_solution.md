## Question

Using

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin x}{x}=1,
$$

compute

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin5x}{x},\qquad \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin2x}{\sin3x}.
$$

In each case, show how you reduce the expression to the fundamental limit.

## Solution

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin5x}{x} = \lim_{x\to0}5\cdot\frac{\sin5x}{5x} = 5 \cdot 1 = 5
$$

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin2x}{\sin3x} = \lim_{x\to0}\frac{2x \cdot \frac{\sin2x}{2x}}{3x \cdot \frac{\sin3x}{3x}} = \lim_{x\to0}\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{\frac{\sin2x}{2x}}{\frac{\sin3x}{3x}} = \frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{1} = \frac{2}{3}
$$
