## Question

Compute

$$
\lim_{x\to3}\frac{x^2-4x+3}{x-3}.
$$

Explain why the value of the function at $x=3$ is not needed to compute the limit.

## Solution

$$
\lim_{x\to3}\frac{x^2-4x+3}{x-3} = \lim_{x\to3}\frac{(x-3)(x-1)}{x-3} = \lim_{x\to3}(x-1) = 3 - 1 = 2
$$

The limit describes behavior as $x$ gets arbitrarily close to $3$ with $x \neq 3$, where $x - 3 \neq 0$ allows cancellation and the function value at $x = 3$ is irrelevant.
