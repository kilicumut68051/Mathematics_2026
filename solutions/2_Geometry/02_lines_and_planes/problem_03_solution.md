## Question

Find the equation of the plane through $P=(1,-2,3)$ and perpendicular to $n=(2,1,-1)$. Check the result by substituting the point $P$ and explain the role of the normal vector.

## Solution

$$
2(x - 1) + 1(y + 2) - 1(z - 3) = 0 \implies 2x + y - z + 3 = 0
$$

Check $P(1, -2, 3)$: $2(1) + (-2) - 3 + 3 = 0$
The normal vector is orthogonal to every displacement vector lying in the plane, defining its orientation.
