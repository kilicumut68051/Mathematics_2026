## Question

Analyze the position of

$$
L_1:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,2,1),
$$

$$
L_2:(x,y,z)=(2,0,1)+s(0,1,-1).
$$

If they intersect, give the intersection point. If not, determine whether they are parallel or skew. Justify the classification.

## Solution

Direction vectors: $\mathbf{d}_1 = (1, 2, 1)$, $\mathbf{d}_2 = (0, 1, -1)$ (not parallel).
Intersection check:
$1 + t = 2 \implies t = 1$
$1 + 2t = s \implies s = 3$
$t = 1 + s \implies 1 = 4$ (contradiction)
Classification: Skew lines (neither intersecting nor parallel).
