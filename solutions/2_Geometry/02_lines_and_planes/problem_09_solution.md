## Question

Compute:

1. the distance from $P=(2,3)$ to the line $3x+4y-10=0$,
2. the distance from $Q=(2,-1,3)$ to the plane $2x-2y+z-4=0$.

Compare the two formulas and explain their common connection with the normal direction.

## Solution

1) $d = \frac{|3(2) + 4(3) - 10|}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{|6 + 12 - 10|}{5} = \frac{8}{5}$
2) $d = \frac{|2(2) - 2(-1) + 3 - 4|}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-2)^2 + 1^2}} = \frac{|4 + 2 + 3 - 4|}{3} = \frac{5}{3}$
Both compute the magnitude of the projection of a vector from the point to the line/plane along the unit normal vector.
