## Question

Given

$$
\Pi_1:x+y+z=1,\qquad \Pi_2:x-y=2.
$$

First use the normal vectors to decide whether the planes are parallel. If not, compute the angle between them and explain why the normal vectors alone are enough for this calculation.

## Solution

$\mathbf{n}_1 = (1, 1, 1)$, $\mathbf{n}_2 = (1, -1, 0)$
Not parallel because $\mathbf{n}_2$ is not a scalar multiple of $\mathbf{n}_1$.

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{|\mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2|}{\|\mathbf{n}_1\|\,\|\mathbf{n}_2\|} = \frac{|1 - 1 + 0|}{\sqrt{3}\sqrt{2}} = 0 \implies \theta = \frac{\pi}{2} \ (90^\circ)
$$

The angle between two planes equals the angle between their normal vectors.
