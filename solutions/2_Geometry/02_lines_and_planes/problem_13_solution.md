## Question

Find the point on the plane $x+2y+2z=4$ closest to $P=(3,0,0)$. Give the distance and check that the segment joining the two points is perpendicular to the plane.

## Solution

$\mathbf{n} = (1, 2, 2)$, $P = (3, 0, 0)$
Line: $(3 + t, 2t, 2t)$
In plane: $(3 + t) + 2(2t) + 2(2t) = 4 \implies 3 + 9t = 4 \implies t = \frac{1}{9}$
Closest point: $Q = \left(\frac{28}{9}, \frac{2}{9}, \frac{2}{9}\right)$
Distance: $\|Q - P\| = \left\|\left(-\frac{1}{9}, \frac{2}{9}, \frac{2}{9}\right)\right\| = \frac{\sqrt{1 + 4 + 4}}{9} = \frac{3}{9} = \frac{1}{3}$
Segment $Q - P = \left(-\frac{1}{9}, \frac{2}{9}, \frac{2}{9}\right) = \frac{1}{9}(-1, 2, 2)$, which is parallel to $\mathbf{n} = (1, 2, 2)$ and therefore perpendicular to the plane.
