## Question

Find the intersection point of

$$
(x,y,z)=(1,0,2)+t(1,2,-1)
$$

with the plane $x+y+z=4$. Check the result by substituting it into both descriptions.

## Solution

$$
(1 + t) + (2t) + (2 - t) = 4 \implies 2t + 3 = 4 \implies t = \frac{1}{2}
$$

Intersection point: $\left(\frac{3}{2}, 1, \frac{3}{2}\right)$
Check line at $t = \frac{1}{2}$: $\left(1 + \frac{1}{2}, 2\left(\frac{1}{2}\right), 2 - \frac{1}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{3}{2}, 1, \frac{3}{2}\right)$
Check plane: $\frac{3}{2} + 1 + \frac{3}{2} = 4$
