## Question

For

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,-1,0),\qquad \Pi:x+y+z=2,
$$

determine whether the line intersects the plane at one point, is parallel to it, or lies in it. Explain the role of the dot product of the direction vector and the normal vector.

## Solution

Direction vector $\mathbf{d} = (1, -1, 0)$, normal vector $\mathbf{n} = (1, 1, 1)$.
$\mathbf{d} \cdot \mathbf{n} = (1)(1) + (-1)(1) + (0)(1) = 0$.
Point $(1, 1, 0)$ on $L$: $1 + 1 + 0 = 2$ (lies in $\Pi$).
Result: The line lies in the plane. $\mathbf{d} \cdot \mathbf{n} = 0$ shows $\mathbf{d}$ is parallel to the plane, and since a point of $L$ is in $\Pi$, the whole line is in $\Pi$.
