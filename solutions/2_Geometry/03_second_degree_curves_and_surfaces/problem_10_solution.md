## Question

Compare

$$
z=x^2+y^2
$$

and

$$
z=x^2-y^2.
$$

For each surface, describe the cross-sections $x=0$, $y=0$, and $z=c$, then make a qualitative sketch.

## Solution

$z = x^2 + y^2$ (elliptic paraboloid):
- $x = 0$: $z = y^2$ (parabola opening up)
- $y = 0$: $z = x^2$ (parabola opening up)
- $z = c$: circle $x^2 + y^2 = c$ of radius $\sqrt{c}$ ($c > 0$), point $(0, 0, 0)$ ($c = 0$), empty ($c < 0$).

$z = x^2 - y^2$ (hyperbolic paraboloid):
- $x = 0$: $z = -y^2$ (parabola opening down)
- $y = 0$: $z = x^2$ (parabola opening up)
- $z = c$: hyperbola $x^2 - y^2 = c$ ($c > 0$), hyperbola $y^2 - x^2 = -c$ ($c < 0$), lines $y = \pm x$ ($c = 0$).
