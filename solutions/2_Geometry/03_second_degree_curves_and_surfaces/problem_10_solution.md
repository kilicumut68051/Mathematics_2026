z = x^2 + y^2 (elliptic paraboloid):
x = 0: z = y^2 (parabola)
y = 0: z = x^2 (parabola)
z = c: circle x^2 + y^2 = c (c > 0), point (0, 0, 0) (c = 0), empty (c < 0)

z = x^2 - y^2 (hyperbolic paraboloid):
x = 0: z = -y^2 (parabola)
y = 0: z = x^2 (parabola)
z = c: hyperbola x^2 - y^2 = c (c > 0), hyperbola y^2 - x^2 = -c (c < 0), lines y = +-x (c = 0)
