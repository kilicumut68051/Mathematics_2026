## Question

For

$$
\frac{x^2}{16}-\frac{y^2}{9}=1,
$$

find the intercepts with the $x$-axis, the asymptotes, and make a sketch. Explain why the asymptotes describe the behavior of the curve far from the center.

## Solution

$x$-intercepts: $(\pm 4, 0)$
Asymptotes: $y = \frac{3}{4}x$ and $y = -\frac{3}{4}x$
As $|x| \to \infty$, $\frac{y}{x} = \pm \frac{3}{4}\sqrt{1 - \frac{16}{x^2}} \to \pm \frac{3}{4}$, so the branches approach the lines $y = \pm \frac{3}{4}x$.
