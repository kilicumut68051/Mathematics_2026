## Question

Reduce

$$
x^2+y^2+z^2-4x+2y-6z-2=0
$$

to canonical form. Find the center and radius. Give two points on the sphere and check them in the equation.

## Solution

$$
(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 16
$$

Center: $(2, -1, 3)$, radius: $r = 4$.
Point 1: $(6, -1, 3) \rightarrow 4^2 + 0 + 0 = 16$; $36 + 1 + 9 - 24 - 2 - 18 - 2 = 0$.
Point 2: $(2, 3, 3) \rightarrow 0 + 4^2 + 0 = 16$; $4 + 9 + 9 - 8 + 6 - 18 - 2 = 0$.
