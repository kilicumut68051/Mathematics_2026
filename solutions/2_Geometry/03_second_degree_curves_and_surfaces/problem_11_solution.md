x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 1 (hyperboloid of one sheet):
z = 0: circle r = 1
z = +-1: circle r = sqrt(2)
z = +-2: circle r = sqrt(5)
Connected surface for all z with radius sqrt(1 + z^2).

x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = -1 (hyperboloid of two sheets):
z = 0: empty
z = +-1: points (0, 0, +-1), r = 0
z = +-2: circle r = sqrt(3)
Two disconnected sheets for |z| >= 1 with radius sqrt(z^2 - 1).
