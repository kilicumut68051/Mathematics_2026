## Question

Compare

$$
x^2+y^2-z^2=1
$$

and

$$
x^2+y^2-z^2=-1.
$$

For several values $z=c$, check whether the cross-section exists and what its radius is. Based on this, describe qualitatively the difference between the surfaces and make sketches.

## Solution

$x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = 1$ (hyperboloid of one sheet):
- $z = 0$: circle $r = 1$
- $z = \pm 1$: circle $r = \sqrt{2}$
- $z = \pm 2$: circle $r = \sqrt{5}$
Connected surface for all $z$ with radius $\sqrt{1 + z^2}$.

$x^2 + y^2 - z^2 = -1$ (hyperboloid of two sheets):
- $z = 0$: empty
- $z = \pm 1$: points $(0, 0, \pm 1)$, $r = 0$
- $z = \pm 2$: circle $r = \sqrt{3}$
Two disconnected sheets for $|z| \ge 1$ with radius $\sqrt{z^2 - 1}$.
