## Question

Recognize the curves

$$x^2+y^2=9,$$

$$\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1,$$

$$y^2=8x,$$

$$\frac{x^2}{9}-\frac{y^2}{4}=1.$$

For each one, identify the most important features and make a sketch. Explain which parts of the equation let you recognize the type of curve.

## Solution

1. $x^2 + y^2 = 9$: Circle centered at $(0, 0)$ with radius $r = 3$. Equal positive coefficients on $x^2$ and $y^2$.
2. $\frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$: Ellipse centered at $(0, 0)$ with semi-axes $a = 4$ (along $x$) and $b = 3$ (along $y$). Sum of squared terms with unequal positive denominators equal to $1$.
3. $y^2 = 8x$: Parabola with vertex $(0, 0)$, axis of symmetry $y = 0$, opening right ($p = 2$). Only one variable is squared ($y^2$) while the other is linear ($x$).
4. $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$: Hyperbola centered at $(0, 0)$, vertices $(\pm 3, 0)$, asymptotes $y = \pm \frac{2}{3}x$. Difference of squared terms equal to $1$.
