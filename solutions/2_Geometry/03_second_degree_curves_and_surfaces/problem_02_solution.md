## Question

Reduce

$$
x^2+y^2-6x+4y-3=0
$$

to canonical form. Find the center and radius, then substitute two simple points from the circle into both forms of the equation.

## Solution

$$
(x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 16
$$

Center: $(3, -2)$, radius: $r = 4$.
Point 1: $(7, -2) \rightarrow (7 - 3)^2 + (-2 + 2)^2 = 16$; $49 + 4 - 42 - 8 - 3 = 0$.
Point 2: $(3, 2) \rightarrow (3 - 3)^2 + (2 + 2)^2 = 16$; $9 + 4 - 18 + 8 - 3 = 0$.
