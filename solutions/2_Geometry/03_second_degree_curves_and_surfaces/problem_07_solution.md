## Question

Determine the type of object described by:

1. $4x^2+9y^2=36$,
2. $x^2-y^2=1$,
3. $x^2-4y=0$,
4. $x^2+y^2+1=0$.

In the last case, explain why there are no real points.

## Solution

1. $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$: Ellipse.
2. $x^2 - y^2 = 1$: Hyperbola.
3. $x^2 = 4y$: Parabola.
4. $x^2 + y^2 + 1 = 0$: Empty set. Since $x^2 \ge 0$ and $y^2 \ge 0$ for all real $x, y$, $x^2 + y^2 + 1 \ge 1 > 0$.
